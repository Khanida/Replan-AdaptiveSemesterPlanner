A plan that earns its attitude.

  1. Add your courses. Set credit hours, exam dates, and topic outlines, or try the demo.
  2. Set realistic ground rules. Workload defaults to 45 study hours per credit. Adjust this estimate to suit your courses.
  3. Generate the roadmap. Work spreads across available days before each exam. Each day rotates through courses, one unfinished topic per course. Courses that do not fit get first turns the next day. Work that cannot fit before exams is reported as unscheduled.
  4. Show up and check in. Complete, miss, or reset individual sessions and whole days. Missed sessions are rebuilt automatically; completed work is preserved.
  5. Keep the receipts. Inspect course progress, topic timelines, daily workload, and replan history. Download your daily PDF checklist with completion boxes and notes. Backups and CSV tools are under Plan backup and recovery; calendar export remains available.

Your plan autosaves in this browser when storage is available. Use Plan backup and recovery in Study roadmap to move devices or restore your plan. Download PDF creates an A4 checklist for each study day, with continuation pages when needed. The roast coach is local, optional, and adjustable.

CSV files from v1.2 can be opened with their .csv.meta.json companion file. Without metadata, exam dates and credits are inferred from the schedule and should be reviewed.
